## Problem

What I originally thought existed:

Golfers finish a round frustrated, then waste range time because they do not know **what to practice next**. Tip overload and random ball-beating replace a clear plan.

---

## Hypothesis

**Original hypothesis:** Golfers don’t know what to practice after a bad round.

If ShotPlan asks what went wrong and returns a short, coach-led session with one swing thought, golfers will practice with more focus and leave Round Ready.

---

## Version 1

Version 1 ships a complete loop:

**Check in → Coach Brief → Guided challenges → Round Ready**

It includes curated drills, diagrams, real-setup photos, a calm coaching voice, an on-device journal, a library, and beta feedback — without accounts or video analysis.

## Key Insights

Editable summaries (refine as research lands):

1. **One bad round isn’t enough** — improvement stories span patterns across rounds.
2. **Golfers think in patterns** — not only isolated “slice today” moments.
3. **Most golfers already know their weakness** — the hard part is what to do next, consistently.
4. **Practice isn’t always the problem — transfer is** — range success ≠ course trust.
5. **Mental game matters** — calm priorities beat tip stacks.
6. **Decision making matters** — what to ignore is as important as what to train.
